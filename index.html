SpotLab Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

SpotLab respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use the SpotLab tournament poker training app.

Information You Provide

When you use features such as Analyze a Hand, you may enter poker hand information for analysis. This information is used to provide the requested feature and improve your experience with SpotLab.

Information Stored on Your Device

SpotLab may store limited app preferences on your device, such as whether you have completed the introductory onboarding screen.

Third-Party Services

SpotLab may use third-party service providers to support certain app features, such as generating hand analysis. Information required to provide those features may be processed by those services.

Feedback and Email

If you choose to send feedback, SpotLab opens your device's email application. Any information you include in that email is provided voluntarily by you.

Selling Personal Information

SpotLab does not sell your personal information.

Children's Privacy

SpotLab is not intended for children under the age required to consent to digital services in their jurisdiction.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as SpotLab adds or changes features. The updated date above will reflect the latest version.

Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be sent to spotlabpoker@gmail.com.
