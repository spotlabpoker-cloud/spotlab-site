SpotLab Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

SpotLab is a poker training and educational application designed to help users practice tournament poker decisions and analyze poker hands.

Information You Provide

When you use the Analyze a Hand feature, you may enter poker hand histories, poker situations, or strategy questions. This information is transmitted for the purpose of generating your requested analysis.

Please do not include sensitive personal information in poker hand descriptions or strategy questions.

How We Use Information

Information submitted through the hand analysis feature is used only to provide the requested poker analysis and improve the functionality of the SpotLab experience.

Artificial Intelligence Processing

SpotLab may use third-party artificial intelligence services to process poker hands and strategy questions. Information submitted for analysis may be transmitted to these services solely for the purpose of generating the requested response.

Subscriptions and Payments

SpotLab offers optional auto-renewable subscriptions through Apple's In-App Purchase system. SpotLab does not receive or store your payment card information. Purchases and subscription management are handled by Apple.

Local App Data

SpotLab may store certain information locally on your device, such as daily analysis usage counts, training progress, and app preferences. This data is used to operate app features and improve your experience.

Tracking and Advertising

SpotLab does not sell personal information and does not use your information for cross-app advertising or tracking.

Children's Privacy

SpotLab is not specifically directed toward children. Users should use the app in accordance with applicable age requirements and App Store policies.

Data Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information transmitted through the app. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
